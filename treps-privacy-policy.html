🦖 TReps Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

TReps ("we", "the app") is a social learning platform. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

1. Data We Collect

Account & profile (stored on your device)

  • Display name, avatar (emoji or Rexy T-Rex image), app settings (theme, text size, reminders).

Learning progress (stored on your device)

  • Quiz answers, accuracy, study streaks, XP, weak-spot lists, daily practice minutes.

Squad / social data (stored in the cloud, Firebase Firestore)

  • Squad names, invite codes, and membership lists.
  • Scores and encouragement messages ("cheers") you choose to share with a squad.
  • Battle challenges, results, and quiz-night participation.
  • Your display name and avatar as shown to squad members.

Push notifications (stored in the cloud, Firebase)

  • A push token for your device, so we can send reminders and battle updates you asked for.

What we do NOT collect

  • No precise location. No contacts. No email or phone number. No payment information (the app is free and ad-supported).

2. Advertising

The app shows ads (banner and limited interstitial). Our ad configuration requests child-directed treatment (COPPA flag), non-personalized ads, and a "G" content rating. Ad providers may collect device identifiers as described in their own policies.

3. Children (COPPA)

TReps may be used by students under 13 (e.g. high-school civics study). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is listed above (display name chosen by the user, study progress, squad interactions). Parents or guardians may request review or deletion of a child's data via the contact below.

4. How We Use Data

  • To run the app: track progress, run squads/battles, send reminders you enabled.
  • To improve the app: aggregated, anonymized usage statistics (e.g. daily active users).
  • We do not sell personal data.

5. Data Sharing

  • Squad data is visible to members of that squad (people you invited or who joined with your invite code).
  • Push tokens are used only to deliver your notifications.
  • We do not share data with third parties except: Firebase/Google (cloud hosting), and ad providers (as described in section 2).

6. Data Retention & Deletion

  • On-device data: delete anytime via Settings > "Delete my account and all data". This also deletes your push token and removes you from your squads. Scores and messages you already shared remain visible to other squad members.
  • Cloud data: to request full deletion of cloud records tied to you, contact us (below) from the device/account in question.
  • We retain data only as long as your account exists.

7. Security

Cloud data is protected by Firebase security rules: writes require authentication and squad content is readable only by squad members. No system is perfectly secure, but we apply industry-standard protections.

8. Changes to This Policy

We will update this page when the policy changes and note the new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: treps.support@gmail.com
