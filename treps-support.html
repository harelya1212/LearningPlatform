TReps Support

Need help with TReps, found a bug, or want to request a test we do not have yet? We read everything.

Fastest: the in-app form

Open TReps and go to Settings > Send feedback / request a test. The form opens your mail app with everything filled in - feedback, a bug report, or a test request - addressed to us. Nothing is sent until you tap send there.

You can also tap Request this test on the Tests screen when a search finds no match.

By email

Write to us at treps.support@gmail.com.

It helps to include:

  • What you were doing when the problem happened.
  • The test name and, for a wrong answer, the question.
  • Your device type (iPhone / Android / web) if the problem is visual.

Data and privacy questions

For data deletion requests or privacy questions, email the same address. See also the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
